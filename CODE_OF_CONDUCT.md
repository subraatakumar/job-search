# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and constructive community.

Expected behavior includes clear communication, good-faith collaboration, respect for different backgrounds and experience levels, and useful technical feedback.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, and deliberately disruptive behavior.

Report concerns privately to the repository maintainer. Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation when needed to keep the community safe.
